ABOUT;

Nan Jiang, also known as Erin, is an interdisciplinary artist and designer whose practice spans physical computing, generative systems and visual design. She graduated from Goldsmiths, University of London, with a Master’s degree in Computational Art. Her work investigates the subtle interplay between physical sensors and generative digital organisms, algorithmic ecosystems, and the boundary between organic and synthetic forms.

Pulse of the COSMOS

This installation turns its attention to cosmic rays, using a solidified ‘foam’ structure to lend this abstract natural force a fateful, material form. The theme is rooted in a core scientific principle: cosmic rays imprint carbon-14 into all living organisms. The installation uses C++ and openFrameworks to monitor cosmic ray data in real time, transforming supernova explosions into silent physical transformations. Controlled via Arduino, the artist steps back, allowing the cosmos to dwell directly within the installation.

the cosmos is just indifferent

This research project presents an interactive installation titled The Cosmos is Just Indifferent, which explores the philosophical tension between individual intrinsic value and external social evaluation. Drawing inspiration from Carl Sagan’s cosmic perspective and H.P. Lovecraft’s Cosmicism, the work posits that the "starstuff" within humans possesses an inherent worth—akin to stellar nuclear fusion—that remains independent of, and is fundamentally indifferent to, human observation. Through this subversion of traditional interactive paradigms, the project challenges the anthropocentric dominance in digital art. It asserts that the most profound state of being is a self-contained "supernova" that refuses to grant others the right of judgment, ultimately reflecting the radical indifference of the universe as a form of existential liberation.

Galdrastafir

Galdrastafir is an interactive installation exploring the intersection of physical tactility and digital mysticism. Using the 24 Elder Futhark runes as a medium, each symbol is deconstructed into geometric vectors. The interaction is distilled into a single act: selecting a rune and placing it upon the altar. As the physical pieces settle, their digital coordinates collide and intertwine within a virtual void. Through this silent alignment, the algorithm initiates a ritual of reconstruction—rotating and merging fragmented strokes into a unique protective Icelandic stave.

Mimic Octopus

I utilized TouchDesigner to create intricate octopus textures and integrated Arduino with a TCS3200 color sensor. The sensor captures the colors of the audience in real-time to manipulate visual parameters within TouchDesigner, which are then projected back onto the octopus model, simulating the biological camouflage of a mimic octopus.

CollidE

Based on the relationship between AI and humanity in the digital age, 'CollidE' explores how AI has ascended to a 'god-like' status in modern society. As individuals increasingly lose self-autonomy to algorithmic echo chambers, they become devotees of big data. The garment design draws inspiration from biblical depictions of angels and divinity—pale, rational, and overwhelmingly powerful.

Smart Collar for Therapy Dog

Nowadays, therapy dogs play an ever-increasing role in society by healing humans, especially those with psychological problems, but with little concern for the mental health of therapy dogs. My design is to help therapy dogs by preventing their psychological problems, caring for their physical and mental health, and getting a sustainable therapy dog programme.

PhysiARound

At a time when science is developing extremely rapidly, there are many ordinary people who have a great sense of distance from cutting-edge physics, making it difficult for them to access and feel the beauty of physics. Through this product, I hope to reduce ordinary people's sense of severance from obscure physics, and build a world of perceived physics that can be used by everyone to communicate with each other. Physics is no longer a carnival for a few people, everyone has the right to explore this universe we live in.
