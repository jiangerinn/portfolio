CollidE

Based on the relationship between AI and humanity in the digital age, 'CollidE' explores how AI has ascended to a 'god-like' status in modern society. As individuals increasingly lose self-autonomy to algorithmic echo chambers, they become devotees of big data. The garment design draws inspiration from biblical depictions of angels and divinity—pale, rational, and overwhelmingly powerful.

Mimic Octopus

I utilized TouchDesigner to create intricate octopus textures and integrated Arduino with a TCS3200 color sensor. The sensor captures the colors of the audience in real-time to manipulate the visual parameters within TouchDesigner, which are then projected back onto the octopus model, simulating the biological camouflage of a mimic octopus.

Galdrastafir

Galdrastafir is an interactive installation exploring the intersection of physical tactility and digital mysticism. Using the 24 Elder Futhark runes as a medium, each symbol is deconstructed into a set of underlying geometric vectors. The interaction is distilled into a single, deliberate act: selecting a rune and placing it upon the altar. As the physical pieces settle, their digital coordinates collide and intertwine within a virtual void. Through this silent alignment, the algorithm initiates a ritual of reconstruction—rotating and merging the fragmented strokes into a unique, protective Icelandic stave (Galdrasta/ir). By stripping away complex interfaces, the work seeks to rediscover the sacred, hushed power of ancient rituals within the precision of electronic logic.
